HamiltonNet Hermes Privacy Policy

Purpose and users

HamiltonNet Hermes is a private personal assistant used by its owner. It is not offered as a public service.

Google information accessed

With the owner's authorization, the assistant can access Gmail messages and metadata, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts within the granted OAuth permissions.

How information is used

Information is used to provide personal assistance, including email summaries, reminders, calendar management, document processing, and personal reference notes. Gmail is operated as an ingest-only integration: the assistant is instructed not to send, reply to, or forward email.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials and retained personal information are stored in the owner's privately administered computing environment, not on this public website.

Relevant information may be transmitted to configured third-party AI providers for processing and to connected services, such as Discord, to deliver assistant responses. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and advertising

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. This public website does not display connected account information.
